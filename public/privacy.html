Shadow Locks

Privacy policy · updated 6 November 2026

The short version: Shadow Locks collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. Your progress stays on your headset, and we never see it.

Who we are

Shadow Locks is a puzzle game for Meta Quest made by Hamid Siddiqui, an independent developer. It runs as a web app and installs from the Meta Horizon Store. This policy covers the game and this website.

What the game saves

The game saves your progress and settings (the boxes you have opened, your daily box streak, the lessons you have finished, and choices such as box size and which hand you use) in the browser storage on your headset that belongs to the game's site. It never sends them anywhere. We cannot see them, and nobody else can either.

Hands, head and controllers

While you play, the game reads your hand, head and controller positions from the headset, so it can place the box in your lap and cast shadows. This happens on the headset, in the moment. None of it is recorded, saved or sent.

The network

The first launch downloads the game's files. After that the game plays offline and only looks for updates. The files come from Cloudflare, our web host. Like every web host, Cloudflare handles standard request data to deliver them: your IP address, and your browser's name and version. We do not see or keep that data. Cloudflare's own policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The game shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking of any kind, and opens no other website.

Deleting your data

Everything the game saves is kept by the headset's browser for the game's site, shadowlocks.pages.dev. To delete it, clear the game's data in your headset's settings: the app's data, and the browser's site data for shadowlocks.pages.dev. If you play in Quest Browser, clearing that site's data is all it takes. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Shadow Locks is meant for players aged 13 and over. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Purchases

This version is free and has no purchases. If a paid unlock comes later, Meta will handle the payment through the Meta Horizon Store under Meta's own privacy policy, and we will never receive your payment details. This page will say so before that happens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
