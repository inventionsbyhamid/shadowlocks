Shadow Locks

Privacy policy · updated 10 November 2026

The short version: Shadow Locks collects nothing about you. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no cookies. Your progress stays on your device, and we never see it.

Who we are

Shadow Locks is a puzzle game for Meta Quest made by Hamid Siddiqui, an independent developer. It runs as a web app and installs from the Meta Horizon Store. On Meta Quest you play with your hands or with Touch controllers. Its web page also opens on an Android phone, where the game shows in augmented reality as a preview. This policy covers the game, on any device, and this website.

What the game saves

The game saves your progress and settings (the boxes you have opened, your daily box streak, the lessons you have finished, and choices such as box size and which hand you use) in the browser storage on your headset or phone that belongs to the game's site. It never sends them anywhere. We cannot see them, and nobody else can either.

Hands, head, controllers and camera

While you play, the game reads where things are, so it can place the box and cast shadows: on a headset, your hand, head and controller positions; on a phone, where the phone is and where you touch the screen. This happens on your device, in the moment. None of it is recorded, saved or sent.

The game shows your room through your device's own view of it: the headset's passthrough, or on a phone the camera view that the browser shows behind the game through WebXR, the web's standard for augmented reality. That is why a phone's browser may ask to use the camera. The game does not record, save or send any of those images.

The network

The first launch downloads the game's files. After that the game plays offline and only looks for updates. The files come from Cloudflare, our web host. Like every web host, Cloudflare handles standard request data to deliver them: your IP address, and your browser's name and version. We do not see or keep that data. Cloudflare's own policy is at cloudflare.com/privacypolicy.

The game shows no ads, uses no analytics or tracking of any kind, and opens no other website on its own. On a computer or a phone, its web page offers Open on my Meta Quest: only if you choose it, it takes you to Meta's own page that sends the game's link to your headset.

Sharing a result

When a box opens, the game shows your result: the box or the day, your time, your hints and a tile for each lock. In the headset it is a card you can take a screenshot of. On the game's web page it is a short text with a link, which you can share or copy. The game sends nothing itself: the text goes only where you choose to paste or share it. Share links hold only a box number or a day, a time, a hint count and the game's version number. There is no name, no account and nothing about your device in them.

Deleting your data

Everything the game saves is kept by the headset's browser for the site the app opens: shadowlocks.pages.dev, or shadowlocks-judge.pages.dev for the Competition build. To delete it, clear the game's data in your headset's settings: the app's data, and the browser's site data for that site. If you play in Quest Browser, or in a phone's browser, clearing that site's data is all it takes. There is nothing on our side to delete.

Children

Shadow Locks is meant for players aged 13 and over. It collects no personal information from anyone, children included.

Purchases

This version is free and has no purchases. If a paid unlock comes later, Meta will handle the payment through the Meta Horizon Store under Meta's own privacy policy, and we will never receive your payment details. This page will say so before that happens.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will appear on this page with a new date.
